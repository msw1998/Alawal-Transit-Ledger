Who It's For

Built for How Freight Actually Works

Not a generic spreadsheet. Not a corporate ERP. RouteBook is designed around the daily reality of running trucks in Pakistan.

Freight Dispatchers

You run 2–5 trips daily per driver. RouteBook lets you log each trip's income, fuel, tolls, and commission in under a minute — and shows total profit before the driver returns.

Business Owners

Own one truck or twenty. RouteBook shows your daily and monthly P&L at a glance. Know exactly which clients owe you, which vendors were paid, and how much cash each driver is holding.

Office Teams

Give staff Editor access to enter daily trips, while you keep Admin control. Invite via email or create username + password accounts directly — no IT required.

Features

Everything a Freight Business Needs

From the first trip of the day to the monthly profit report — every tool is already here.

Daily Entry Form

Log every driver's day in one structured form — multiple trips, trip income, per-trip expenses (diesel, royalty, commission, toll plaza), payment mode (cash / credit / partial), driver advance, company expenses, and misc expenses. The live summary updates as you type.

CF Pricing Calculator

Toggle the CF button on any income or expense field. Enter Price/CF and Total CF — the amount calculates instantly. Works on both trip income and individual expenses.

Driver Cash Balance

Previous cash carries forward automatically. Log advances, cash collected from clients, and transfers between drivers. Always know who holds how much.

Invoice Generator

Create professional invoices with your logo, BOM table, and tax. Export a clean A4 PDF — no browser headers, no print dialog. Saved as the invoice number.

Monthly Dashboard & Analytics

See your entire month in one view — total income, expenses, and profit in visual charts. Break down by client or driver. Export everything to Excel for your accountant with one click. Browse any past day in Daily View with full trip detail.

Client & Vendor Payments

Record payments received from clients and payments made to vendors — separate from trip income. Cash and credit tracked independently.

Role-Based Access

Admin sees P&L, dashboard, invoices, and settings. Editor enters daily trips only. Create accounts with username + password — no email required.

Business Settings

Upload your logo, set address and phone. These appear on every invoice automatically. Manage team members and change business name anytime.

How It Works

One Day in RouteBook

Your daily workflow, from first trip to final tally — done in minutes.

1

Open New Entry

Pick the driver and date. Previous cash balance loads automatically — nothing to calculate.

2

Log Trips & Expenses

Add each trip: destination, client, income, payment mode. Attach every expense — diesel, royalty, toll, commission. Use CF toggle for cubic feet pricing.

3

See Profit Instantly

The summary panel updates live as you type. Income, expenses, profit, and driver's remaining cash — always visible, always current.

4

Save the entry. Browse any past day in Daily View. Pull up the full month in Dashboard. Generate an invoice when the client needs one.
